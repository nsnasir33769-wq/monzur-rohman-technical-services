ABOUT OUR COMPANY

Welcome to Monzur Rohoman Technical Services

At Monzur Rohoman Technical Services, we specialize in providing high-quality commercial kitchen equipment, maintenance services, and custom solutions. Whether it's dishwashers, convection ovens, deep fryers, chillers & freezers, or cold room installations and maintenance, we ensure your kitchen runs smoothly. We also offer a wide range of commercial equipment spare parts and stainless steel fabrications, designed to meet the unique needs of your business. Our commitment is to deliver exceptional service, reliability, and performance every step of the way.

We supply a wide range of catering equipment tailored to suit all operational scale requirements and budgets. From specialized kitchen machinery tools to full-scale commercial cooking suites, we offer complete custom design, layout execution, and preventive maintenance agreements.
